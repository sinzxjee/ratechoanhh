## Expanded meme pools

The 20 generated cat portraits are stored as separate `meme-cat-extra-01.webp` through `meme-cat-extra-20.webp` files. The five generated dog portraits are separate `meme-dog-extra-01.webp` through `meme-dog-extra-05.webp` files. These were extracted from the original generated contact sheets with the white gutters removed, then optimized as WebP. The browser loads each portrait as a complete image instead of positioning a sprite sheet. Together with the five earlier memes per animal, the pools contain 25 cats and 10 dogs. Goat and otter each retain their existing set of five photos.
